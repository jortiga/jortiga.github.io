Titan Slasher

Tap to teleport-slash a colossal stone titan. Chain combos, break runes, rank up.

About the game

Every tap is a teleport slash. Tap the titan, and your hero blinks across the sky and cuts it with a burst of violet light. Tap faster and the slashes stack into a storm.

TAP. SLASH. REPEAT.

  • Every tap teleports your hero and lands a painted slash where you tapped
  • Build your combo for bigger arcs, critical hits and auto Omni Finishers
  • Hammer the glowing rune weak points before they fade

GET STRONGER

  • Earn coins every fight and upgrade your tap, slash and critical hits
  • Climb the leaderboard and beat your personal best

Art is AI-assisted and hand-directed.
