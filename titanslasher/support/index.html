Common questions

How do I play?

  • Tap FIGHT to challenge today's titan (each fight uses energy, which refills over time)
  • Tap the titan: every tap teleports your hero and lands a slash where you tapped
  • Keep tapping to build your combo and unleash finishers
  • Hammer the glowing rune weak points before they fade for big damage
  • Spend shards on chests and upgrade your gear between fights

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so progress can't be moved to another phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it. Your leaderboard nickname and scores are stored on our leaderboard server.

How do I remove my leaderboard entry?

Email us your nickname and roughly when you last played, and we will delete it.

Are there ads or purchases?

There are optional rewarded ads. The Android version also sells shard packs through Google Play; the iOS version has no in-app purchases.
