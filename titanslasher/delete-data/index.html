Delete your Titan Slasher data

Titan Slasher by Studio 3

Titan Slasher has no accounts. The data we store outside your phone is your online leaderboard entry and, if you used guilds, your guild membership.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jzmile69@gmail.com with the subject "Titan Slasher delete my data".
  2. Include your leaderboard nickname and roughly when you last played.
  3. We delete your entry within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • The random player ID the game created on your device
  • Your nickname
  • All your scores on the leaderboard
  • Your guild membership and join history. If you still lead a guild, that guild is removed too (to keep it for the others, leave it in the game first: leadership passes to another member)

Nothing is kept after deletion. We keep no backups of deleted leaderboard entries.

Data on your phone

Your coins, upgrades, equipment and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
