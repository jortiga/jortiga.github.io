Titan Slasher Privacy Policy

Studio 3 · Effective 28 November 2026

Titan Slasher is a tapping action game for phones made by Studio 3. This policy explains what information the game handles. It covers the Android version and the iOS version.

What stays on your device

Titan Slasher has no accounts and no sign-in. Your coins, upgrades, equipment, best scores and your sound setting are saved on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online leaderboard and guilds

To show the leaderboard and guilds, the game sends these to our server:

  • a random ID that the game creates on your device (it is not your phone's advertising ID or any hardware ID),
  • the nickname you choose (please don't use your real name),
  • your scores and a few game statistics sent with them (such as best combo, number of taps, upgrade levels and your hero level),
  • if you use guilds: your guild membership and, if you found a guild, the guild name you give it.

Other players can see your nickname, scores, guild name and, inside your guild, your level and when you last played. We use this data only to run the leaderboard and guilds. The server is hosted by our provider Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. The data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

To have your entry deleted, follow the steps on Delete your data.

Advertising

Titan Slasher shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When the ad service is used, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required you are asked for consent first, and on iPhone the app asks permission before your activity is used for tracking. You can say no and still play. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

The Android and iOS apps may send anonymous usage and gameplay events to Google Analytics 4, a Google service, so we can see how the game is used and improve it: for example when the app is opened or left and for how long, whether a rewarded ad was shown, failed or finished, and similar gameplay events. Each event carries a random identifier that the game creates on your device (not your advertising ID, and not the leaderboard ID or your nickname), your platform, the game version, your language and your screen size. Google also receives your IP address, which it can use to estimate your approximate location. We do not use this data to identify you or to show you ads. See Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The Android version offers optional shard packs sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The iOS version currently has no in-app purchases.

Game updates

The app may download updated game files from GitHub. As with any download, GitHub receives your IP address.

Children

Titan Slasher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Data handled for advertising is shared with Google and its advertising partners, and analytics events are processed by Google, as described above. Supabase (leaderboard and guilds) and GitHub (game updates) receive the data described above as our service providers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
