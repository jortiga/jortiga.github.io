Slash Titans Privacy Policy

Studio 3 · Effective 26 November 2026

Slash Titans is a tapping action game for phones made by Studio 3. This policy explains what information the game handles. It covers the Android version and the iOS version.

What stays on your device

Slash Titans has no accounts and no sign-in. Your coins, upgrades, equipment, best scores and your sound setting are saved on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online leaderboard

To show the leaderboard, the game sends three things to our leaderboard server:

  • a random ID that the game creates on your device (it is not your phone's advertising ID or any hardware ID),
  • the nickname you choose (please don't use your real name),
  • your scores.

Other players can see your nickname and scores on the leaderboard. We use this data only to run the leaderboard. The server is hosted by our provider Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. The data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us your nickname and roughly when you last played, and we will remove it.

Advertising and analytics

This version of Slash Titans shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.

Children

Slash Titans is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
