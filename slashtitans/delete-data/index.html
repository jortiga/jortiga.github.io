Delete your Slash Titans data

Slash Titans by Studio 3

Slash Titans has no accounts. The only data stored outside your phone is your online leaderboard entry.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jzmile69@gmail.com with the subject "Slash Titans delete my data".
  2. Include your leaderboard nickname and roughly when you last played.
  3. We delete your entry within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • The random player ID the game created on your device
  • Your nickname
  • All your scores on the leaderboard

Nothing is kept after deletion. We keep no backups of deleted leaderboard entries.

Data on your phone

Your coins, upgrades, equipment and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
